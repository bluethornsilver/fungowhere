# Practical information

`venue-info.js` records the following official pages checked on 11 October 2026. Only these three venue records have this check; the remaining catalogue is inherited and should not receive the same checked label.

- [Singapore Botanic Gardens visitor information](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/visit/general-info/): gardens hours and free entry, separate National Orchid Garden tickets, entrance/MRT context, wheelchair routes and maintenance reminder. Ordinary visit booking remains unconfirmed.
- [URA Singapore City Gallery plan your visit](https://www.ura.gov.sg/get-involved/singapore-city-gallery/visit/): free entry, Monday–Saturday hours, Sunday/public-holiday closure, booking for groups above 20, access facilities and the announced 2026–2027 revamp closures.
- [NParks Fort Canning Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/fort-canning-park): 24-hour grounds, Cox Terrace wheelchair entry, nearby stations and Park Use Request instruction for group activities. Entry price was not reconfirmed; the old free-entry catalogue claim is explicitly distinguished.
